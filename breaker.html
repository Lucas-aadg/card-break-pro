Step 1 of 7
Welcome to Card Break Pro
Your owner has set up your account and you're ready to start breaking. This quick guide will walk you through everything you need to know — it only takes 2 minutes.
This guide will only show once. Take 2 minutes to read it carefully.
📅
Step 2 of 7
Your Schedule
Your owner assigns shifts to you directly. Check the My Schedule tab to see your upcoming shifts, times, and any notes from your owner.
Your owner controls the schedule. If a shift looks wrong, reach out to them — not through this app.
⏱️
Step 3 of 7
Clocking In
When your shift starts, clock in using the Clock In / Out button in the app. The system automatically tracks your hours from that moment.
Always clock in before you start breaking. Hours are only tracked when you're clocked in.
Don't forget to clock out when your shift ends — your payout depends on accurate hours.
📦
Step 4 of 7
Logging Breaks
This is the most important part of your job. After each break closes:
1. Enter the stream key your owner gives you
2. Select each product that was in the break
3. Enter the quantity sold for each
4. Hit Submit Break
Submit every break as it closes. Don't batch them — the running total needs to stay live for your owner.
✅
Step 5 of 7
Closing Out
When the stream ends, use Close Out Stream. The system automatically:
  • Calculates your commission
  • Records your payout
  • Closes the stream in the system
Nothing is manual. Don't calculate your own pay — the system handles it based on what you submitted.
💰
Step 6 of 7
Your Payouts
After each stream closes, your commission and hours are recorded. You can view your full payout history and commission breakdown in the My Payouts tab anytime.
If a payout looks wrong, talk to your owner. Only they can adjust records.
Your pay rate and commission structure were set by your owner when they added you to the team.
🚀
Step 7 of 7
You Are Ready
That's everything. You now know how to:
  • Check your schedule
  • Clock in and out
  • Log each break correctly
  • Close out a stream
  • View your payouts
Start breaking.
