FAQ

GOT QUESTIONS.

Does this work with Whatnot, TikTok Shop, and YouTube?
Yes. CardBreakPro works alongside any streaming platform. You run your break wherever you want — we handle the business backend. Platform agnostic by design.
How long does setup take?
Most owners are fully set up in under 10 minutes. Add your team, configure commission rates, and start your first break. No technical knowledge required.
Can my breakers see my financials?
No. Role-based access means each team member sees only what they need. Breakers see their personal commissions. Only you see the full financial picture.
How many team members can I add?
Starter supports up to 5 breakers, 3 sorters, and 1 manager. Pro supports up to 15 breakers, 12 sorters, and 3 managers. Empire has no limits on team size. You can upgrade at any time and your data carries over instantly.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
Your data is yours. We provide a full export before any account closure. We don't hold data hostage and we never delete your records without explicit confirmation.
Is my financial data secure?
Yes. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-standard AES-256. We never share your financial data with third parties and we don't sell user data — ever.
