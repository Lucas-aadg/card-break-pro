Step 1 of 6
Welcome to Card Break Pro
You've been added as a sorter. Your job is to process and ship the cards that sell during breaks. This guide walks you through how the system works.
This guide only shows once. Take 2 minutes to read it.
📋
Step 2 of 6
Your Sort Queue
Your main screen is the Sort Queue. When a stream ends, streams that need sorting appear here automatically.
You do not add tasks yourself. Tasks are created by the system when streams close.
Each task shows the stream name, how many breaks it had, and when it was posted.
⏱️
Step 3 of 6
Clocking In
Before you start working, clock in using the Clock In button. Your hours are tracked from that moment until you clock out.
Always clock in first. Your hourly pay depends on accurate clock times.
Clock out as soon as your shift ends — do not leave it running.
📦
Step 4 of 6
Starting and Completing a Sort
Click Start Sorting on a task to claim it. Only one sorter can work a task at a time. When you're done:
1. Pull and package the cards for that stream
2. Ship them out
3. Click Mark Complete
Do not mark complete until the order is actually shipped. Your owner tracks this data.
💰
Step 5 of 6
Your Pay
Your pay is calculated automatically based on your hours clocked. You can view your full pay history and shift breakdown in the My Pay tab.
If something looks wrong, talk to your owner. They control your rate and can adjust records.
🚀
Step 6 of 6
You Are Ready
You now know how to:
  • Check your sort queue
  • Clock in and out
  • Start and complete sort tasks
  • View your pay
Go sort some cards.
