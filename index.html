How it works

ONE STREAM, START TO FINISH.

Built inside a working break operation that runs multiple Whatnot accounts every week. Every screen exists because a stream night demanded it.

01 — DURING THE STREAM

The breaker runs the break from their phone.

Pick the account you're streaming on, tap the boxes you're opening — any categories, giveaways included — and type the platform's running total. CardBreakPro derives this break's revenue, fees, profit and the per-spot price you needed to break even. Stock comes off the shelf count on its own.

  • No per-order data entry. One number per break.
  • Only that account's products show; nothing selected is ever hidden by a filter.
  • Over-use is flagged, never silently trimmed — counts stay honest.
  • "Repeat last break" in one tap.

FAQ

GOT QUESTIONS.

Does this work with Whatnot, TikTok Shop and YouTube?
The break calculator, close-out, payroll, inventory, scheduling and exports work for any platform — you enter the running total and the final payout, we do the rest. Buyer intelligence is built on Whatnot packing-slip exports today; other platforms' order exports are on the roadmap.
Do my breakers have to enter every sale?
No. They type the platform's running total once per break; CardBreakPro derives that break's revenue from the difference. At close-out they enter the final payout figure and the app computes the real fee and net. That's the whole data-entry job.
Which pay setups are supported?
Breakers: commission on net (rate per breaker, or per category), hourly, salary, per-break, or hourly + commission and salary + commission. Sorters and managers: hourly or salary. Tips can be recorded too. Payroll runs by day, week, two weeks, month or a custom range, prorates shifts that cross the edges, and subtracts what you've already paid.
What's a packing slip and why does it matter?
After a stream, Whatnot lets you export a packing-slip PDF — every order, every buyer. Drop it into CardBreakPro at close-out (or later, from "Slips needed") and it becomes your buyer CRM: spend per buyer, first-timers, whales, who's gone cold. Big files are fine; uploads in parts merge instead of overwriting.
Can my breakers or sorters see my financials?
No. Roles are strict: breakers see their own pay and the streams they run; sorters see the sort queue and their hours; managers see operations but not pay settings or payroll. Only the owner sees the full financial picture.
How many people and accounts can I add?
As many as you want. One plan, unlimited seats and unlimited Whatnot accounts — breakers, sorters and managers are all included. The price doesn't change as you grow.
How long does setup take?
About ten minutes: add your accounts, add your products with costs and counts, invite your team, set each person's pay. Your first break can be logged the same night.
What happens to my data if I cancel? Is it secure?
Your data is yours: every report exports to CSV at any time, and cancelling is one click from Billing. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, isolated per organization, and never sold or shared. Questions: hello@cardbreakpro.com.
